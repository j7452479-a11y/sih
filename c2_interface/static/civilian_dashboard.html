PHYSICS RESEARCH HOW AUTOMOTIVE EV LIDAR WORKS (VS AERIAL UAV)
An Electric Vehicle operates at ground elevation (0.5m - 2.0m) with strictly forward-facing and perimeter sensors. It has no access to top-down / aerial views.
1. EGO-CENTRIC PERSPECTIVE ONLY

Unlike UAVs flying at +30m with top-down views, an EV looks horizontally along the road. All points are recorded in the vehicle's body frame (X_right, Y_forward, Z_up). Top-down views are physically non-existent for an EV.

2. GRAZING-ANGLE ROAD ARCS

At depression angle α < 0, ground hit distance is d = h / tan(-α). At grazing angles (≤2°), small angle steps jump tens of meters down the road, creating expanding elliptical rings on the asphalt.

3. RADIAL OCCLUSION SHADOW CONES

Opaque obstacles (parked delivery van, concrete pillars) block laser rays, casting a dark radial shadow cone on the asphalt. Zero laser returns penetrate behind the obstacle until a pedestrian steps out into line-of-sight.

4. SE(3) SUSPENSION NOSE-DIVE COMP

Under hard braking (-7.5 m/s²), front suspension compresses, pitching the vehicle down by -2.5°. 6-DOF IMU applies P_corr = R_pitch(-θ) * P_raw to prevent treating flat road as an uphill obstacle wall.

5. NATIVE SPHERICAL RANGE-VIEW

Rather than slow 3D grids, automotive edge hardware (NVIDIA DRIVE Orin) projects returns to a 2D Range Image Matrix (32 channels × 64 beams) mapping pitch vs azimuth. RangeNet++ runs 2D convolutions at 60+ FPS.

6. CAPPED MLS LONGITUDINAL CLEARANCE

For highway underpasses and tunnels, Capped Multi-Level Surfaces separate road surface [0.0m, 0.2m] from concrete ceiling [3.2m, 4.0m], proving +1.60m safe headroom without false blockage.
