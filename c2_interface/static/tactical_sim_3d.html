Rather than slow 3D grids, automotive edge hardware (NVIDIA DRIVE Orin) projects returns to a 2D Range Image Matrix (32 channels × 64 beams) mapping pitch vs azimuth. RangeNet++ runs 2D convolutions at 60+ FPS.
